# NAV and Unit Allotment Lag

When you make a mutual fund payment or an auto-debit takes place, your bank account is debited first. However, mutual fund units are not credited instantly.

1. **Funds Settlement:** Money moves through the banking clearing system (NPCI / NACH / UPI) to the mutual fund asset management company (AMC).
2. **NAV Timing:** Mutual fund Net Asset Value (NAV) is computed and declared once each business day after the financial markets close (around 9:00 PM to 11:00 PM).
3. **Unit Allotment:** Once funds reach the fund house and the day's closing NAV is published, units are generated. Under Indian market guidelines, this typically takes 1 to 2 business days after bank debit.
4. **Weekends and Holidays:** Non-business days (Saturdays, Sundays, and bank holidays) do not count as settlement days.

During this interval, your order status displays "In progress" or "Allotment in progress". Your money is with the clearing agency or AMC, not lost.
