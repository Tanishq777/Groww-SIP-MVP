# Mutual Fund Unit Custody and Platform Safety

When you invest in mutual funds through Groww, your money and accumulated units are held under strict SEBI regulatory segregation:

1. **Where Units are Held:**
   - Mutual fund units are issued directly by the Asset Management Company (AMC) and registered with regulated Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs) such as CAMS or KFintech, or credited to your central depository demat account (CDSL/NSDL).
   - Groww acts as a SEBI-registered broker and execution platform. Groww does not hold your investment money or fund units in its own corporate accounts.

2. **What Happens if a Broker Shuts Down:**
   - If Groww or any execution platform shuts down, your mutual fund units remain completely secure with the fund house (AMC) and RTA/depository.
   - Your ownership is mapped directly to your PAN and Folio number. You can always view, manage, switch, or redeem your units directly on the AMC portal or via CAMS/KFintech without depending on Groww.

3. **Regulatory Protection:**
   - Under SEBI investor protection regulations, client assets are segregated from broker assets, ensuring investor capital cannot be liquidated or seized for broker liabilities.
