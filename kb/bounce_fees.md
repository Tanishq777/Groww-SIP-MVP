# Bank Return Charges vs Platform Fees

When an automated SIP debit attempt fails because of insufficient account balance, investors often notice a deduction labeled as a "NACH Return Charge" or "ECS Bounce Fee" on their bank statement.

1. **Who Levies the Charge:** 
   - This fee (typically ₹250 to ₹500 plus GST) is debited directly by **your own bank** (e.g., HDFC Bank, SBI, ICICI Bank).
   - Banks levy this administrative penalty for processing an unpaid standing instruction.
2. **Groww Charges ₹0:** 
   - Neither Groww nor the mutual fund AMC charges any penalty or fee for a missed or bounced SIP debit.
3. **No Automatic Penalizing Retries:** 
   - To protect investors from compounding bank bounce charges, the platform does not continuously auto-retry bounced debits in the same cycle.
4. **How to Avoid Bank Bounce Charges:**
   - Setting your SIP debit date 3 to 5 days after your guaranteed salary credit date.
   - Using the "Move Date" or "Pause SIP" feature at least 4 business days before the scheduled debit if cash flow is constrained.
