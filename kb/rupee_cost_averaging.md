# Rupee-Cost Averaging Concept

**Rupee-Cost Averaging (RCA)** is a mathematical characteristic of investing a fixed rupee amount on a regular schedule across different market prices.

1. **How It Operates Mechanically:**
   - When NAV is lower (e.g., during a market correction), your fixed instalment amount purchases **more units**.
   - When NAV is higher, that same fixed instalment amount purchases **fewer units**.
2. **Average Cost Formula:**
   $$\text{Average Cost per Unit} = \frac{\text{Total Money Contributed}}{\text{Total Units Accumulated}}$$
3. **No Return Guarantees:** Rupee-cost averaging does not guarantee profit or eliminate the risk of capital loss in a sustained downward trend. It is simply a mathematical mechanism that blends your entry price over time without requiring you to time market peaks or troughs.
