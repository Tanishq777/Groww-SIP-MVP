# Mutual Fund Units and NAV

Mutual fund ownership is measured in **units**, similar to how physical commodities are measured in grams or litres.

1. **NAV (Net Asset Value):** The price of one single unit of a mutual fund scheme on a given day. It is calculated by taking the scheme's total portfolio assets minus liabilities, divided by total outstanding units.
2. **Units Calculation:** 
   $$\text{Units Allotted} = \frac{\text{Instalment Amount}}{\text{Applicable NAV}}$$
   Units in India are credited with high mathematical precision (typically up to 3 decimal places, e.g., 19.231 units).
3. **Current Portfolio Value:**
   $$\text{Current Value} = \text{Total Units Allotted} \times \text{Current NAV}$$
4. **Permanent Units:** Once allotted, your units belong to you until you choose to redeem them. Even if NAV fluctuates, your number of owned units remains unchanged.
