# Lock-ins, Exit Loads and Charges

Whether an investment can be redeemed immediately or carries fees depends strictly on the specific mutual fund category and scheme.

1. **Lock-in Periods:**
   - Most open-ended equity and debt funds have **no mandatory lock-in period**, allowing redemptions at any time.
   - However, **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)** tax-saving mutual funds carry a statutory **3-year lock-in** under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. During these 36 months, units cannot be redeemed or switched out.
   - Solution-oriented funds (like Retirement or Children's funds) may have a 5-year lock-in.

2. **Exit Loads (Redemption Charges):**
   - An exit load is a fee charged by the fund house if you redeem your units before a specified holding duration (commonly 1% if redeemed within 12 months for equity funds).
   - Some liquid, overnight, or specific index funds carry **zero exit load**.
   - Exit loads apply only when you **redeem (sell)** units, not when you stop future SIP instalments.

3. **Stopping an SIP vs Redeeming Units:**
   - Modifying debit dates, reducing instalment amounts down to ₹100, or cancelling future SIP debits does not incur platform penalties from Groww.
   - When you stop an SIP, units already accumulated simply remain invested in the fund until you decide whether or when to redeem them.

4. **Verify Scheme Information:**
   - Always refer to the scheme's official **Scheme Information Document (SID)** or **Key Information Memorandum (KIM)** for the exact lock-in period, exit load structure, and tax treatment applicable to your specific fund.
