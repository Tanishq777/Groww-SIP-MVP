# Options When You Need to Adjust Your SIP

A Systematic Investment Plan is a flexible arrangement, not a binding loan or locked contract. If your financial circumstances or cash flow change, you have three neutral choices:

1. **Move Debit Date:** 
   - You can shift your monthly debit date (e.g., from the 2nd to the 10th) so it aligns with when salary is deposited.
2. **Reduce Instalment Amount:** 
   - You can reduce your monthly commitment down to the scheme's minimum instalment floor (often ₹100). This maintains your habit without straining your monthly budget.
3. **Pause for 1 to 3 Cycles:** 
   - A pause instructs the bank not to debit for upcoming selected months.
   - Pausing does not harm your credit score, does not incur any fee, and does not sell or forfeit existing units. Your milestones simply rest, and counting resumes on the next successful debit.
4. **Cancel SIP:** 
   - Cancelling stops future auto-debits from your bank account.
   - Units purchased up to that point remain in your folio account. Future exit loads, redemption feasibility, or lock-in rules depend on the specific fund scheme document.
